# Hezky česky – user guide

Hezky česky (shown in the app header as “Czech for 3rd grade”) is a web app for practising Czech at 3rd-grade level: listed words (vyjmenovaná slova), paired consonants,
parts of speech and more. Children answer questions, collect points and climb ranks named after FC Barcelona players.
Parents see their children's results.

The app runs in a browser on a computer, tablet or phone. Nothing needs to be installed.

**Language:** switch the interface between Czech and English in the top right corner (**CS / EN**). The questions are
always in Czech; the explanation after each answer follows the interface language. This guide uses the English
button names. In Czech mode the same buttons have Czech names, listed in brackets.

---

## For parents

### 1. Create a parent account

1. Open the app's address. On the **Log in** page (Přihlášení), click **Sign up** (Registrace).
2. Under **Who is signing up?**, choose **I am a parent** (Jsem rodič).
3. Fill in a **Username** and your **Password** twice (at least 6 characters), then click **Create account**.

After signing up you land in **For parents** (Pro rodiče). No e-mail address is needed. The username has 3–30 characters
and may contain only letters, digits, dot, dash and underscore.

### 2. Add your child

**Option A – create an account for your child** (easiest for younger children)

In **For parents**, fill in **Create a child account** and click **Create account**. The child is linked to you right
away and logs in with that username and password.

**Option B – your child already has an account**

1. The child logs in and opens **My results** (Moje výsledky). At the top they see a **Code for parents**
   (Kód pro rodiče), 8 characters long.
2. In **For parents**, enter the code under **Link an existing child account** and click **Link**.

Upper/lower case and spaces in the code don't matter. The code changes after use, so nobody else can reuse it.

**A second parent** creates their own parent account and links the child the same way (option B). The child finds the
new code in **My results** again.

### 3. Follow the results

**For parents** shows an overview of all your children: number of answers, success rate and the rank in each level
(🔒 means the level is still locked). **Details** (Podrobnosti) shows:

- **Questions done**, correct and wrong answers, success rate;
- **By level**: rank with its player, and how many questions are done;
- **By topic**: where your child does well and what needs practice;
- **Mistakes to fix**: questions most recently answered wrong, with the correct answer;
- **Recent exercises**;
- **Answer history (last 100)**: exactly what your child typed, and when.

You only see children linked to your account. Other parents cannot see your children.

### 4. Forgotten password

- **Child's password:** in **For parents**, open **Details** for the child. Under **New password for …**, enter the new
  password twice and click **Save password**. The child then logs in with the new password.
- **Parent's password:** contact the app administrator (whoever sent you the link).

### Tips

- On a tablet or phone, add the page to the home screen (browser menu → **Add to Home screen**). The child can then open
  it like an app.
- The browser keeps you logged in for 30 days, so always log out (**Log out**, top right) on a shared computer.
- We recommend 10–15 minutes a day, which is one or two exercises.

## For children

### Logging in

On the **Log in** page, type your username and password and click **Log in**. A parent can create your account, or you
can sign up yourself with **Sign up** → **I am a pupil** (Jsem žák / žákyně).

### How to practise

1. In **Practice** (Procvičování), pick a level:
   - **Level 1 – easy**
   - **Level 2 – medium**
   - **Level 3 – hard**
2. Click **Start** to get 10 questions. To practise only one topic, open **Practise one topic only** and pick it.
3. Answer:
   - If there are buttons under the question, click the right answer.
   - If there is a text box, type your answer and click **Check** (or press Enter). Letters with diacritics
     (á, č, ě, ř, ů …) are on the buttons below the box.
4. You see **Correct! 🎉** or **That's not right 🙁** straight away, with a short explanation 💡.
   Continue with **Next question**.
5. At the end, click **See result** to see how you did and whether you moved up a rank.

**When typing an answer:**
- upper and lower case don't matter;
- diacritics do matter: "dum" is not "dům";
- if you fill in several letters at once, separate them with a comma, for example `y, i`.

### Every question only once

You get each question only once, even if you answer it wrong. That way you work through every question in a level.

Your mistakes are not lost: the start page shows **Mistakes to fix** and a **Fix mistakes** button. There you can
repeat the questions you got wrong and correct them.

### Unlocking levels

Level 1 is open from the start. The next level unlocks once, in the previous level, you have:
- answered at least **80 %** of the questions;
- with a success rate of at least **70 %**.

A locked level shows a padlock 🔒 and how much is still missing.

When you have answered every question in a level, you see **Level complete!**.

### My results

**My results** shows:
- how many questions you have done and how many were correct;
- your mistakes;
- your **Code for parents**.

## Points and ranks

In each level you have your own **rank from 0 to 10**, and each rank belongs to an FC Barcelona player:

| Rank | Player | Rank | Player |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Alejandro Balde | 6 | Lamine Yamal |
| 1 | Pau Cubarsí | 7 | Carles Puyol |
| 2 | Fermín López | 8 | Xavi Hernández |
| 3 | Gavi | 9 | Andrés Iniesta |
| 4 | Raphinha | 10 | **Lionel Messi** |
| 5 | Pedri | | |

- A correct answer gives **+1 point**, a mistake **−0.5 points**.
- Points also count in **Fix mistakes**, so fixing a mistake wins the points back.
- Every tenth of the level's questions that you have "in points" moves you one rank up. Rank 10 (Lionel Messi) needs
  almost everything correct.
- On the start page, each level shows your current player and how many points you need for the next one.

## Frequently asked questions

**I see "That question was already answered".**
The answer was already sent, for example after a double click or the browser's Back button. Nothing is lost; just
continue with the current question.

**"Too many failed login attempts".**
After 5 wrong passwords, logging in is blocked for 15 minutes. Wait, or ask a parent to set a new password for the child.

**"Too many accounts were created from this connection".**
At most 5 accounts can be created per hour from one connection. Please try again later.

**"This code is not valid".**
Check the code in the child's **My results**; it changes after every link. After 10 attempts in an hour, you need to
wait a while.

**"No new questions left here".**
You have answered every question in this level or topic. Try another topic, the next level, or **Fix mistakes**.

**A level has no Start button.**
The level is either locked (🔒) or already complete (🏆).

## Privacy and security

- The app stores only the username, the password and the answers. The password is stored securely, not as readable
  text. No e-mail address or other personal data is required.
- For a child's username we recommend a nickname rather than their full name.
- A child's results are visible only to the child and the parents linked to them.
- The app's address starts with `https://`, so the connection is encrypted.
